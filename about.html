About SchoolMate Pro

Manage Your School Smarter.

SchoolMate Pro is an offline desktop school data management application designed for Indian schools. It brings student administration, attendance, fees, examinations, reports and parent communication together in one application.

Designed for Everyday School Administration

Purpose-focused tools help schools manage their important records and routine work.

Offline Desktop Application

Works without internet. School data is stored on the local computer, without cloud dependency.

Multi-School Management

Manage school sections from one dashboard while keeping student records and fee structures separate for each school.

WhatsApp Integration

Support parent communication through fee reminders, paid fee receipts, attendance alerts and Class Teacher Attendance Link.

Core School Management Areas

Key operational areas covered by the SchoolMate Pro website.

Student Administration

Admissions, student profiles, documents, records and academic-year management.

Academic Operations

Attendance, examinations, marks entry and cumulative or descriptive progress cards.

Fees & Reporting

Fee structures, collection, receipts, outstanding fee details, reports and certificates.
